# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in PalCenter Companion a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Give and accept technical feedback in good faith.
- Focus on what is best for users and the community.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's private information.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publication of private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers through the contact method in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when conduct is inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

This policy applies in project spaces and when an individual publicly represents the project.
